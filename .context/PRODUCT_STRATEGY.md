## Executive Summary

RMGaaS (Resource Management & Governance as a Service) is an enterprise-grade platform for managing professional services workforce allocation, utilization, and governance. Built to replace fragmented spreadsheets with an intelligent, real-time platform.

---

## Vision Statement

> **Transform how professional services firms manage their most valuable asset—their people—by replacing fragmented spreadsheets with an intelligent, real-time platform that maximizes utilization, minimizes bench cost, and enables data-driven workforce decisions.**

---

## Target Market

### Primary Segment

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Company Size** | 1,000 - 5,000+ employees |
| **Geography** | Global (from day 1) |
| **Industry** | IT Services + Engineering Services |
| **Current State** | Using Excel, basic tools, or nothing formal |

### Expansion Path

1. **Phase 1:** IT + Engineering Services
2. **Phase 2:** All Professional Services (legal, accounting, consulting)
3. **Phase 3:** Broader (agencies, studios, creative services)

---

## Competitive Landscape

### Primary Competition

| Competitor Type | Examples | Our Position |
|-----------------|----------|--------------|
| **Modern SaaS Tools** | Float, Runn, Resource Guru, Saviom | Better for large IT services, AI-native |
| **Excel/Spreadsheets** | The incumbent | Real-time, intelligent, scalable |
| **No Formal System** | Tribal knowledge | Structure + visibility |

### Secondary Competition (Occasional)

- Enterprise PSA (Kantata, Planview, SAP)
- In-house built tools
- HRMS add-on modules

### Differentiation Strategy

| Differentiator | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Bench Intelligence** | Best-in-class bench management with AI-driven recommendations |
| **AI-Native Matching** | ML-based resource recommendations with explanations |
| **Enterprise Scale** | Built for 1000+ employees, not just small teams |
| **India/Global Services Fit** | Understands practice structure, bands, bench culture |
| **Speed to Value** | Import Excel, live in hours not weeks |
